## Historical context used in the dashboard

- **Penal convict:** serving compulsory hard labour under prison administration with severely restricted movement; some could live outside prison while remaining under sentence.
- **Exile settler:** required to reside in exile, often after hard labour; could establish a household and livelihood while remaining under supervision and movement restrictions.
- **Exile peasant:** a former settler granted broader rights, including conditional permission to leave Sakhalin for permitted parts of Siberia, while remaining barred from returning to the original homeland.
- **Free-status person:** outside the penal/exile categories, subject to ordinary imperial laws and restrictions associated with individual status. This is not a statement of unrestricted freedom or equal rights.

These are concise contextual summaries based on Chekhov's [Chapter XV](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Остров_Сахалин_(Чехов)/Глава_15) and [census description](https://feb-web.ru/feb/chekhov/texts/sp0/spe/spe-039-.htm?cmd=p), not a complete statutory rights classification. They describe principal categories; they must not be applied automatically to children in the combined groups.
